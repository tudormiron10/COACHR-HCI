# Project Brief

## Scenario

You are the head coach of a top-tier football team. You use a mobile device (tablet/phone) to access real-time data regarding player performance, tactics, possession statistics, and more. During live matches, you also use a pair of special MR glasses to view live tactical data mapped around the pitch and the players (including opponents) — passing lanes, defensive lines, and positioning errors. Moreover, with your special smartwatch, you can quickly push tactical instructions to your bench staff and receive haptic confirmations.

## Target Platforms

Design must be delivered for all three devices below. Each platform has a distinct role in the ecosystem — they are complementary, not redundant.

| Platform | Role | Primary use moment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tablet / Mobile Phone** | Central data hub: analysis, statistics, tactical drawing, dispatch to staff. | Pre-match preparation, half-time review, in-match tactical authoring. |
| **AR Headset** | Live HUD overlaying tactical data on the field and players (including opponents). | Live match observation from the touchline. |
| **Smartwatch** | Glanceable channel to push instructions to staff and receive haptic confirmations. | Mid-action, hands-busy moments during the match. |
